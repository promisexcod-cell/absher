Absher Design Analysis:

Colors:
- Primary Green: #1B7D3E (dark green) or #2E8B57 (sea green)
- Light Green: #4CAF50 or #5CB85C
- Background: White/Light Gray (#F5F5F5)
- Text: Dark gray/black
- Accent: Gold/Yellow for highlights

Layout:
- Clean white background
- Header with logo on right, search and language on left
- Vision 2030 logo in header
- Green banner/hero section
- Card-based service sections
- Login form on the right side
- RTL (Right-to-Left) layout

Typography:
- Arabic fonts (likely Tajawal or similar)
- Clean, modern sans-serif

UI Elements:
- Rounded corners on cards and buttons
- Green primary buttons
- White cards with subtle shadows
- Icon-based navigation
- Service cards with icons

Header Elements:
- Saudi emblem/logo
- Search icon
- Language toggle (Arabic/English)
- Navigation icons (About, Services, etc.)
- Vision 2030 logo
